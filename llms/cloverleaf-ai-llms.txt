# Cloverleaf AI

> Cloverleaf AI is the B2G sales intelligence platform that monitors 45,000+ government organizations and turns public meetings into pre-RFP buying signals, so sellers reach government buyers before the RFP is published.

Cloverleaf AI serves up 50,000+ insights daily across federal, state, local, and education (SLED) agencies. It monitors committee meetings, budget discussions, and procurement activity, then delivers a prioritized, territory-specific brief to each seller every morning, synced to Salesforce. Cloverleaf AI operates at cloverleaf.ai, is a government (B2G) sales intelligence company, and is headquartered in Denver, Colorado.
