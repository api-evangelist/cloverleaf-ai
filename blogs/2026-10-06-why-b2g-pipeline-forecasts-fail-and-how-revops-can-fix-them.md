Commercial RevOps teams know that a healthy-looking pipeline can still miss the quarter. A champion changes roles, finance freezes spending, security review expands, or a rep fails to build consensus across the buying committee. Forecasting requires judgment about both seller execution and the buyer’s ability to move.
