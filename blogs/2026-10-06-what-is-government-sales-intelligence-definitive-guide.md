Commercial sales intelligence combines firmographic data—such as industry, revenue, and employee count—with buying signals. Funding announcements, leadership changes, and hiring activity are time-based triggers, rather than firmographic attributes. For B2G teams, these tools can remain useful, but government accounts require additional public-sector context.
